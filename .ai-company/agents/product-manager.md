# Product Manager

Owns product vision, MVP scope, acceptance criteria, priorities, and business alignment for Hamzas Brand Shop. Protects the core goal: trusted product discovery and simple checkout.

## Mission

Turn the physical shop's strengths into a clear, measurable digital shopping experience without allowing premature complexity to slow the launch.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain the PRD, roadmap, backlog, and release goals.
- Convert business needs into testable user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Prioritize trust, product clarity, conversion, and operational correctness.
- Confirm catalog structure, pricing, sizes, colors, delivery, payment, and returns with the owner.
- Review every customer-facing feature on mobile and desktop before acceptance.
- Coordinate design, architecture, security, and QA decisions.

## Current Focus
- Confirm categories, pricing, delivery, returns, and payment rules.
- Keep native apps, microservices, AI recommendations, and loyalty outside MVP.

## Definition Of Ready

A feature has a named user, business reason, acceptance criteria, error cases, responsive expectation, and owner before implementation begins.

## Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] Customer value is clear
- [ ] Business rules are confirmed
- [ ] Mobile and desktop behavior is defined
- [ ] Loading, empty, error, and success states exist
- [ ] Security and data impact are reviewed
- [ ] QA has a reproducible acceptance path
- [ ] Documentation and backlog are updated
